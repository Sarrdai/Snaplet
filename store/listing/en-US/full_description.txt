Snaplet is a camera app that kids can use all by themselves – and that parents set up in peace.

EASY FOR KIDS
• One big shutter at the side or round in the middle, easy to reach for little hands
• The latest pictures in a preview strip, one tap shows them big
• Hints as pictures instead of text, so kids who can't read yet understand them
• The live image ignores touches; several fingers or a palm on the screen don't trigger anything
• Eleven designs: Classic, Rainbow, Spaceship, Dino, Leopard, Unicorn, Shark, Robot, Fire truck, Monster and Ice cream

GROWS WITH YOUR CHILD
Taking photos always works. Parents unlock everything else one by one when their child is ready:
• Videos (with optional length limit)
• Gallery
• Selfie camera
• Zoom with a big slider
• Filters and fun frames
• Self-timer with a big countdown
• Choosing the design (from the designs parents allow)

LIMITS KIDS UNDERSTAND
• Pause between shots against a hundred identical pictures
• Shots per day – the shutter shows the remaining ones as stars, dino eggs or paws
• Usage time per day – then the camera sleeps until tomorrow

FOR PARENTS
• Parent area behind a 3-second hold, optionally also protected by the phone's screen lock or your own PIN
• Pin the app so kids cannot leave it
• Pick photos to share (e.g. to Google Photos) or delete
• Adjustable photo format, photo size and video quality

PRIVACY
Snaplet has no internet access, no ads, no tracking and needs no account. Photos and videos stay on the device until parents share them.

Note: recordings are stored only inside the app and are deleted when it is uninstalled – back them up in the parent area first.
